🌏 Web3 Proof DAO (Tenchi Ai Project) / Shomei-kun Concept 🌏

🌐 Web3 Proof DAO — Official Overview

Shomei-kun is a blockchain-based proof application designed to preserve records such as photos, videos, audio, text, PDFs, and ideas, while providing evidence of when they were created and that they existed at a particular point in time.

It is being developed to allow individuals to easily register creative works, research records, statements, business records, and other information, and later verify their contents and the time at which they were created.

Registered information can be referenced and shared through certificates and QR codes, supporting an environment in which individuals can manage their own records.

Web3 Proof DAO is being developed as a decentralized community centered on the “democratization of proof.” It aims to establish a social infrastructure in which individuals can register records such as photos, videos, audio, text, and PDFs on a blockchain themselves and demonstrate their authenticity.

Article 1 — Philosophy

The DAO seeks to foster a system of “proof by individuals” that does not depend on centralized authorities, creating an environment in which trust is distributed and everyone has fair access to proof.

Article 2 — Web3 Proof App

The first stage of the DAO is the development and adoption of the Web3 Proof App. Users will be able to register record data on a blockchain with a simple operation, with functions including public/private settings, password protection, and certificates.

Article 3 — Token Design (Founders Allocation)

The founder allocation is set at 15%, consisting of 12% permanently locked and 3% freely available. This is intended to provide a foundation for sharing value as the DAO grows while balancing DAO autonomy with founder stability.

Article 4 — Governance

Decisions will be made through token voting, and important matters will be handled transparently on-chain. The founder retains veto rights to prevent the project from deviating from its fundamental direction.

Article 5 — Contributions and Rewards

Contributions in development, public relations, translation, design, and other areas will be recorded. Contributors may receive tokens or profit distributions according to their level of contribution. A fair incentive model is intended to encourage an autonomous community.

Article 6 — Future Development

Web3 Proof DAO is intended eventually to expand into fields including education, healthcare, government administration, and law, providing infrastructure through which individuals and organizations can handle proof simply and effectively.

🟦 1. Philosophy

Proof should not inherently be something granted by a particular authority or administrator.
Everyone should be able to record, prove, and verify their own acts of creation, actions, and records.
This is our fundamental belief.

Photos, videos, documents, designs, thoughts, actions, and the records of those activities should fundamentally be managed and provable by the person who created them.

Individuals should be able to manage their records easily according to their own intentions and prove and verify them whenever necessary.
We consider this mechanism to be the first step toward the “democratization of proof.”

However, in existing proof structures, many forms of proof depend on administrators and centralized systems, and proof granted after the fact has become common.
Under such structures, it is difficult for individuals to fully manage their own actions and records and prove or verify them under their own responsibility.

Web3 Proof DAO proposes a different approach:
Rather than adding proof afterward, the moment of creation itself is established as the origin of the authoritative record, and its subsequent history is preserved in a structure designed to resist alteration and enable later presentation.

Proof is not something newly granted afterward.
It is the continued preservation of a record established at the moment of creation in a form that can be independently verified.
The concepts of a unique authoritative record, origin generation, and confirmed history form the core structure through which this idea can be implemented.

Web3 Proof DAO aims to create a society in which individuals take responsibility for their own creations and records and can record, prove, and verify them independently.
Our goal is to implement the democratization of proof not merely as a philosophy, but as a functioning social mechanism.

🟩 2. Development History & Current Status

Shomei-kun is being developed as a system that enables individuals to register their own photos, videos, audio, text, PDFs, and other records and subsequently verify and share both the time of creation and the history that follows.

The concept goes beyond simply storing data. The initial registration is established as the origin of the proof, and subsequent reference, re-presentation, disclosure control, and preservation are handled as a continuous process.

Individuals will also be able to determine whether each record is public, restricted, or private, supporting a social infrastructure in which people manage their own records and prove them when necessary.

■ Technical Architecture of Shomei-kun

The 10 registered utility models are not unrelated standalone technologies.
They have been developed as components of a single proof system covering the process from record registration through history verification, re-presentation, disclosure management, storage, and practical use in society.

  1. 1. Record
    Photos, videos, audio, text, PDFs, creative works, research records, business records, and other materials can be registered easily from smartphones and computers.
  2. 2. Establish the Initial Registration
    The initially registered information is established as the starting point of the proof history.
    This creates a structure that distinguishes the original record from information added later.
  3. 3. Connect Subsequent History
    Even when information is added or updated after registration, the original registration point is preserved and subsequent changes can be reviewed as a continuous history.
  4. 4. Re-present and Verify
    Registered records can be presented again through QR codes, URLs, and similar mechanisms.
    The individual or a third party can reference and verify the origin of the record and its subsequent history.
  5. 5. Manage Disclosure
    Each record can be configured as public, restricted, or accessible only to the individual, depending on its purpose and intended use.
  6. 6. Preserve and Use in Society
    The system is designed to integrate with cloud storage, certificates, QR codes, and codes attached to physical objects, supporting applications involving personal records, creative works, research, business documents, and unique physical items.

Overview of the Patent Application

The invention establishes “what was first registered” as the origin of the history for a registered subject and continuously records, manages, and verifies subsequent updates, additions, and corrections while preserving their relationship to that origin.

At the initial registration, a fixed hash value is generated from fixed information relating to the identity of the subject, while a variable hash value is generated from information relating to its initial state. These values are established together as “origin registration information.”

The fixed-hash system retains information concerning the identity of the registered subject. The variable-hash system generates a new hash value whenever the state of the subject is added to or changed, allowing its subsequent history to be managed continuously.

The application also includes additional structures such as a reserve-hash system for managing future information or information different from ordinary state changes; a mechanism for adding correction histories while retaining the original fixed hash; and a multi-point, multi-layer hash structure combining partial hash values for individual information fields with an overall hash value for the complete record.

When necessary, hash values and related information can be recorded in external evidence systems, including blockchains, allowing consistency with registered information and continuity of history to be verified.

The application also includes configurations for generating proof information based on origin registration information, update histories, hash information, and verification results, and presenting that proof as documents, images, PDFs, or other certificates, potentially with QR codes or other reference information.

In simple terms, this invention is intended to go beyond one-time proof of existence.
While retaining information concerning the identity of the subject, it connects the initial registration with subsequent updates, additions, and corrections, enabling the continuity of the history to be confirmed and verified. This is a technology aimed at realizing “continuous history proof.”
